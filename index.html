How to send Kenny a challenge

Use your channel points on the reward called GTA 6 Challenge. It costs 500 points. When it asks you to type something, type only the number of the challenge you want, like 57. Look up any number in the box below, or scroll the whole list.

Want to dress Kenny? Use the Pick My Outfit reward, or pick 1 (Lucia), 2 (Jason), or 3 (both) here. Type Lucia or Jason, the store name, and each piece, like the top, bottoms, and shoes. Example: Lucia, Bargain World, red hoodie, blue jeans, white shoes. Kenny gets 5 minutes to buy it all. Once he has every piece on, a 10 minute timer starts, and he has to wear it the whole time. After that he can wear whatever he wants. Real clothes only, no underwear. Same as challenges: if he dies while shopping, the 5 minutes start over, and if he dies while wearing it, the 10 minutes start over. Deaths show on stream. If he can't get it all in 5 minutes, you get a free slot spin.

Anything that isn't a number from 1 to 1000 gets rejected and you get your points back.

The rules

Kenny picks how long each challenge lasts and sets the timer himself. Every time he dies, the timer starts all the way over. The deaths count shows on stream.

Kenny does your challenge or your outfit when he's ready, not in the middle of something. If he's in a story mission, robbing a store, or anything else he can't stop, your request waits in line. The stream will say IN A MISSION so you know he saw it, and he does it right after. If an outfit timer is running when a mission starts, it pauses until he's back.

If Kenny FAILS your challenge, you get 1 free slot machine spin, saved just for you. Type !freespin in chat whenever you want to play it.

The reward has a 10 minute cooldown for everyone, and each viewer can use it 3 times per stream.

Every challenge says who Kenny has to play as: Lucia, Jason, or both together. No swapping in the middle.

Kenny is in Chapter 2, so every challenge is something he can do right now around Port Gellhorn and the Keys. Most are just for fun, like pool, races (in a rented car or his own), fishing, selling cars to Moses, and goofy driving. The ones with cops are in the COPS section, and nothing goes above 2 stars.
